# Threat Catalog

## Cyber

- Ransomware affecting scheduling and cargo visibility
- Credential theft
- Data integrity failures
- Distributed denial of service
- Operational technology compromise
- Cloud or communications outage

## AI-enabled threats

- Synthetic voice impersonation
- Highly tailored spearphishing
- False operational reports
- Automated influence and rumor amplification
- Data poisoning and manipulated decision support

## Physical and infrastructure

- Flooding
- Power-grid anomalies
- Fuel constraints
- Bridge or rail failure
- Port closure
- Severe weather
- Water-treatment interruption

## Large gatherings and points of interest

Large gatherings increase transportation, medical, public-information, communications, and public-safety demand. They are modeled as planning pressures, not as targets for violence.
