## How contribution credit should be recorded

For each accepted contribution, record:

1. Contributor name
2. Related issue
3. Pull request or commit
4. Files, research, artwork, or scenario content supplied
5. Reviewer
6. Date merged
7. Source or license information when applicable

## Attribution cautions

- Role assignments do not automatically establish authorship.
- Government links do not imply agency sponsorship or endorsement.
- Mentor feedback should be credited as guidance, not as agency approval.
- Original art, sound, writing, code, and research should identify its creator and license.
- Contributors should not upload private, classified, export-controlled, proprietary, or personally identifying information.
