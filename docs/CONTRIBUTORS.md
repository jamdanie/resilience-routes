## How future contribution credit is recorded

For each accepted contribution, record:

1. Contributor name
2. Related issue
3. Pull request or commit
4. Files, research, artwork, scenario content, testing, or documented findings supplied
5. Reviewer
6. Date merged
7. Source and license information when applicable

Initial role assignments that did not result in submitted and incorporated artifacts are not listed as implementation credit. This policy can be updated if additional documented work is later accepted.

## Attribution cautions

- Government links do not imply agency sponsorship or endorsement.
- Mentor feedback is credited as guidance, not as agency approval.
- Original art, sound, writing, code, research, and scenario content should identify its creator and license.
- Contributors must not upload private, classified, export-controlled, proprietary, or personally identifying information.
