# Roadmap

## Current stopping point — 8.0.0

The course and portfolio product is feature-complete. It includes two regional missions, seeded replayable disruption sets, strategic resources and intelligence, live logistics and weather, temporary injects, an operational map, GIS tools, structured learning, contributor-ready JSON content, after-action reporting, and local history.

The project now prioritizes stability and usable content over adding more interface or simulation systems.

## Maintenance track

- Correct reproducible defects.
- Keep dependencies, workflows, and security guidance current.
- Address documented accessibility and browser-compatibility findings.
- Review community injects and regional packs through schemas, validation, tests, and pull requests.
- Preserve fictional-data, privacy, attribution, and licensing requirements.

## Optional future releases

These are separate product decisions, not unfinished requirements:

### Connected facilitator platform

Role-based multiplayer, shared sessions, facilitator controls, and cohort reporting would require an approved backend, identity model, privacy policy, moderation plan, operational owner, and ongoing hosting budget.

### Offline classroom edition

An installable PWA could support unreliable connectivity after service-worker caching, update behavior, and offline report storage are tested.

### Authoring studio

An in-browser editor could preview and export schema-valid injects while the current JSON scaffolding and pull-request workflow remain the supported contribution route.

No optional phase should begin until its owner, audience, acceptance criteria, and maintenance commitment are documented.
