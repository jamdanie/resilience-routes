# Government and Public Learning Resources

These links connect the simulation to authoritative public material. They are provided for education and further research. Check the issuing website for the newest version before relying on any resource professionally.

## Emergency management and resilience

### FEMA National Risk Index
https://hazards.fema.gov/nri/

Community-level natural-hazard risk, expected annual loss, social vulnerability, and resilience information.

### FEMA Resilience Analysis and Planning Tool
https://rapt-fema.hub.arcgis.com/

A public GIS tool for visualizing population, infrastructure, and community-resilience considerations.

### FEMA Preparedness Toolkit
https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/

Planning, exercise, and preparedness resources for emergency-management users.

### Ready.gov Business Emergency Plans
https://www.ready.gov/business/emergency-plans

Continuity planning, crisis communications, emergency response, and IT disaster-recovery guidance.

### Ready.gov Business Resources
https://www.ready.gov/business

Preparedness, incident management, resource management, continuity, and recovery information.

## Transportation and freight

### U.S. DOT National Freight Strategic Plan
https://www.transportation.gov/freight/NFSP

National freight priorities addressing safety, efficiency, resilience, security, innovation, and workforce needs.

### U.S. DOT Freight and Logistics Supply-Chain Assessment
https://www.transportation.gov/supplychains

Transportation vulnerabilities, stakeholder findings, and policy responses for freight resilience.

### U.S. DOT National Transportation Recovery Strategy resources
https://www.transportation.gov/disaster-recovery

Transportation restoration roles and recommendations following major disasters.

### Bureau of Transportation Statistics
https://www.bts.gov/

Public transportation statistics, freight data, maps, and analytical products.

### Freight Analysis Framework
https://faf.ornl.gov/faf5/

Freight-flow data and projections for analysis and planning.

Governance and cybersecurity risk outcomes organized around Govern, Identify, Protect, Detect, Respond, and Recover.
