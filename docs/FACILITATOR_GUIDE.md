# Facilitator Guide

## Audience

Students, emergency managers, cybersecurity professionals, logistics teams, and interdisciplinary groups.

## Suggested session

1. Briefing — 5 minutes
2. Play — 15–25 minutes
3. After-action review — 15 minutes
4. Team reflection — 10 minutes

## Discussion prompts

- Which node became the most important chokepoint?
- When did efficiency conflict with redundancy?
- Which decisions protected life safety but increased cost?
- What information would have changed the decision?
- Which partner should have been involved earlier?
- What capability should be built before the next disruption?

## Team mode without a backend

Assign participants roles and require verbal agreement before committing:

- Incident commander
- Logistics lead
- Cyber lead
- Public-safety lead
- Finance lead
- Intelligence lead
