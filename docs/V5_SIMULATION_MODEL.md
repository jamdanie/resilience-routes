# Version 5 Simulation Model

## Purpose

Version 5 creates a living operational picture without using live tracking or sensitive data. Movement and incidents are simulated locally so every object supports an educational objective.

## Systems

- Logistics movement: ships, aircraft, trains, and trucks
- Cargo priority: medical, food, fuel, electronics, and relief
- Critical infrastructure: transportation, utilities, energy, cyber, water, and warehousing
- Points of interest: hospitals, schools, emergency operations centers, cloud services, and large gatherings
- Threats: cyber, AI-enabled social engineering, weather, infrastructure anomalies, and crowd demand
- Preparedness: cyber teams, logistics coordination, medical surge, public safety, intelligence, and reserve fuel
- Outcomes: resilience, supply health, cyber posture, public confidence, infrastructure health, delay, and mission objectives

## No live data

The moving assets are fictional. Coordinates are schematic percentages within a stylized map and are not real-time locations.
