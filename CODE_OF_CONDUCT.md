# Contributor Code of Conduct

Contributors are expected to communicate respectfully, review work constructively, credit others accurately, and focus disagreements on project needs and evidence.

Do not submit harassment, discriminatory content, private personal information, credentials, confidential records, classified information, proprietary material without authorization, or unlicensed creative assets.

Project maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when behavior or submissions undermine safety, legality, inclusion, or project integrity.
